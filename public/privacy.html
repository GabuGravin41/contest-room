The Contest Room

How your information is used

The Contest Room is the online platform for the Kenya Informatics Olympiad (KIO). This page explains what we collect when you take part, why, and what happens to it. If you are under 18, please read it with a parent or guardian.

What we collect

  • Your details from your registration: name, school, county and candidate number, linked to your contest code.
  • Your answers, saved as you work, and the time you submitted.
  • Activity during the contest: when you opened each problem, the timing of key presses in answer boxes (not a recording of your screen), when you left the page or full screen, and attempts to copy or paste text.
  • Technical details: the IP address and the browser you used each time you entered your code.

We do not use your camera or microphone, and we do not see anything outside this page.

Why we collect it

  • To run the contest and keep your answers safe if your connection drops.
  • To mark your answers and publish results.
  • To keep the contest fair: activity records help us check whether anyone cheated. A record that looks unusual is reviewed by people; it is never used on its own to disqualify anyone.

Who can see it

Only the KIO organising team. Markers see your answers without your name, school or code. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Your information is stored in a secure online database used by the organisers, with backup copies kept by the organisers. Activity records and IP and browser details are deleted once results are final and any reviews are closed. Your name, school and results are kept as part of the olympiad's records, for example to invite qualifiers to later rounds.

Your choices

You, or your parent or guardian, can ask to see the information we hold about you, to correct it, or to have it deleted, by contacting the KIO organisers through your school or the contact details on your invitation. Deleting your information after the contest may mean your result can no longer be confirmed.
